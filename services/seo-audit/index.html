SEO Audit

A deep 100+ point SEO audit covering technical issues, content gaps and authority signals — with a prioritized action plan you can actually follow.

You cannot fix what you cannot see. Our SEO audit examines your site across 100+ checkpoints: technical health, page speed, mobile experience, content quality, keyword targeting and backlink profile.

You get a clear report — not jargon soup. Every issue is explained, prioritized by impact and paired with a fix. Critical problems first, quick wins highlighted, long-term strategy mapped.

Whether you implement fixes yourself or hire us to do it, you will know exactly where your site stands and what moves the needle.

An audit is only as good as its prioritization. Anyone can list 200 issues; we tell you which five will move rankings this quarter. Our reports separate critical blockers from nice-to-haves, estimate the effort for each fix, and flag anything your developers might break while fixing. You also get a competitor benchmark — because knowing you are slow means little until you see your rivals are slower, or faster.

  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals review
  • Content and keyword gap analysis
  • Backlink profile health check
  • Prioritized action plan by impact
  • Plain-language report, no jargon
