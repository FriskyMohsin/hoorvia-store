Keyword Research

Data-backed keyword research that finds terms you can actually rank for — mapped to pages with search intent and difficulty analysis.

Ranking starts with targeting the right keywords. We research your niche and deliver a keyword map: high-opportunity terms, realistic difficulty assessments and exactly which page should target what.

We analyze search intent behind every keyword, so you attract buyers — not just browsers. Competitor gaps reveal terms your rivals missed. Long-tail opportunities give newer sites a real path to traffic.

You get a spreadsheet you can act on immediately, plus a briefing call to walk through the strategy.

Most keyword research stops at volume and difficulty. We go further: mapping each keyword to a funnel stage, so your content attracts researchers, comparers and buyers in the right proportions. We also identify the keywords your competitors rank for accidentally — thin content you can dethrone with something genuinely better. The final map includes content briefs for priority terms, so writers know exactly what to produce. Target smarter, rank faster, waste nothing.

[ What you get ]

  • High-opportunity keyword discovery
  • Search intent analysis per keyword
  • Difficulty and competition scoring
  • Keyword-to-page mapping
  • Competitor gap identification
  • Strategy walkthrough call included
