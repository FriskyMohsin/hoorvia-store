Data Analysis & Results Writing

Turn raw data into clear findings. Statistical analysis plus professionally written results sections with tables and interpretation.

Data means nothing until it is analyzed and explained. We run the right statistical analyses on your dataset and write a results section that presents findings clearly, accurately and persuasively.

Descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, regression, thematic analysis for qualitative data — matched to your research design. Results are written in proper academic style with correctly formatted tables and figures.

You also get a plain-language explanation of what the numbers mean, so you can defend your findings with confidence.

The wrong statistical test is worse than no test — reviewers spot it instantly. We start from your research questions and design, select the appropriate analyses, check assumptions, and only then run the numbers. Every table follows APA-style conventions, every finding is reported with the statistics reviewers expect, and limitations are stated honestly. You receive the analysis output files too, so everything is reproducible and defensible. When your defense or review comes, you will know your numbers cold.

[ What you get ]

  • Right tests chosen for your research design
  • SPSS, Excel, R or Python analysis
  • Publication-style tables and figures
  • Results written in academic voice
  • Plain-language interpretation included
  • Methodology notes for your chapter

[ FAQ ]

What data formats do you accept?
Excel, CSV, SPSS files or survey exports. Messy data is fine — we clean it first.
Do you do qualitative analysis too?
Yes. Thematic analysis, coding and narrative synthesis for interviews and open responses.
Will you explain the results to me?
Always. You get a clear walkthrough so you understand and can defend every finding.
