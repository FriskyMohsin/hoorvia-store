3D Logo Design

Take your brand into the third dimension. Premium 3D logos with depth, materials and lighting — for web, video and print.

Flat is forgettable. A 3D logo gives your brand physical presence — depth, material, light. The kind of mark that looks incredible on a website hero, in a video intro or embossed on packaging.

We model your logo in true 3D: extruded letterforms, metallic or glass materials, studio lighting and camera angles that show it at its best. Already have a flat logo? We dimensionalize it. Starting fresh? We design flat and 3D together.

Delivered as high-resolution renders from multiple angles, plus turntable animation options for video use. Your brand, with gravity.

Great 3D logos start as great flat logos — dimension should amplify the mark, not rescue a weak one. We refine the underlying design first, then build in 3D with materials that match your brand personality: brushed metal for established trust, glass for transparency, neon for energy. Lighting is treated like photography, with rim lights and reflections composed deliberately. The renders work everywhere from website heroes to trade show screens.

[ What you get ]

  • True 3D modeling, not fake effects
  • Premium materials: metal, glass, neon
  • Studio lighting and camera work
  • Multiple angle renders included
  • Turntable animation option
  • 4K high-resolution delivery

[ FAQ ]

I already have a flat logo. Can you make it 3D?
Yes. Dimensionalizing existing logos is a standard project for us.
What files do I receive?
High-res PNG renders, transparent versions and optional MP4 turntable animation.
Can the 3D logo be animated for video intros?
Yes. Logo sting animations for intros and outros are available as an add-on.
