Reel & Video Editing

Professional reel and short-form video editing with hooks, captions, transitions and trending audio. Content engineered to be watched.

The first two seconds decide everything. Our reel editing is built around retention: killer hooks, dynamic cuts, animated captions and pacing that keeps thumbs frozen.

Send raw footage — phone clips are fine. We return polished vertical videos with subtitles, transitions, sound design and trending audio matched to your niche. Talking head, b-roll, product demos, UGC style — all covered.

Consistent posting needs consistent editing. We offer per-video and monthly batch plans so your content never stops.

Retention editing is a craft: the hook in the first second, a visual change every two to three seconds, captions for sound-off viewers, and pacing that builds to a payoff. We study what is working in your niche right now — not last year's trends — and cut your footage to match. Raw footage stays raw in the best way: authentic, not overproduced. On Reels, polished-but-boring loses to real-and-riveting every time.

  • Animated captions and subtitles
  • Trending audio matching

  • Vertical format for all platforms
