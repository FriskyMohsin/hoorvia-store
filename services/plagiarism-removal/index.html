Plagiarism Removal

High similarity score? We rewrite flagged sections into original, natural academic text while keeping your meaning intact. Originality report included.

A high similarity score can sink a submission. We take your flagged document and rewrite every problematic section into original, natural academic prose — preserving your meaning, data and citations.

This is not word-spinning. We genuinely rephrase and restructure at the sentence and paragraph level, so the text reads freshly written rather than mechanically altered. Technical terms and quoted material are handled correctly.

You get the rewritten document plus a fresh originality check. Your ideas stay yours. Only the wording changes.

Similarity scores punish legitimate writing too — common phrases, methodology descriptions, and properly cited material all inflate the percentage. We distinguish real problems from false positives, rewriting only what genuinely needs it while documenting what can stay. The result is a lower score achieved honestly, with your academic integrity fully intact. Many clients come to us after automated paraphrasers mangled their meaning; we fix both the score and the damage.

[ FAQ ]

What similarity score can you achieve?
We target under 10 percent excluding references and quotes, which satisfies most journals and universities.
Do you just use paraphrasing tools?
No. Every section is rewritten manually by an academic writer. Tools cannot preserve meaning reliably.
Can you work from a Turnitin report?
Yes. Share the similarity report and we focus rewriting exactly where it is needed.
