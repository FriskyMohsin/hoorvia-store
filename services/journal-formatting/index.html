Journal Formatting (APA, MLA, IEEE)

Desk-reject-proof formatting for journal submission. References, headings, tables and layout matched exactly to your target journal.

Many papers get desk-rejected before review for one reason: formatting. We format your manuscript exactly to your target journal's author guidelines — references, headings, tables, figures, margins, everything.

APA, MLA, IEEE, Harvard, Chicago, Vancouver or a specific journal's house style. We check every in-text citation against the reference list, fix ordering and punctuation, and lay out tables and figures to spec.

Submit with confidence knowing the editor's first impression is a clean, compliant manuscript.

Every journal's guidelines hide small traps: reference ordering rules, table footnote styles, heading capitalization, author affiliation formats. We have formatted for enough journals to know where authors slip up. Beyond the visible layout, we check the logic — every citation cited, every reference cited, figure numbers sequential, supplementary files labeled. Editors process compliant manuscripts faster, which means your paper reaches reviewers sooner. Spend your energy on research; let us handle the rulebook.
