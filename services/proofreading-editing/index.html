Proofreading & Editing

Line-by-line proofreading and editing for academic papers. Grammar, clarity, flow and formatting polished to publication standard.

Great research deserves flawless presentation. Our proofreading and editing service takes your draft and returns it clean, clear and publication-ready — without changing your meaning or voice.

We fix grammar, punctuation and syntax. We tighten wordy sentences, improve flow between paragraphs and check that your citations and references are consistent. For non-native English writers, we also smooth out phrasing so your ideas land with full force.

Track changes included, so you see every edit and stay in control. Fast turnaround available for deadline pressure.

Editing is invisible when done well — readers simply feel the text is clear and professional. That invisibility takes deliberate work: untangling dense sentences, fixing article and preposition errors common in academic ESL writing, standardizing terminology, and ensuring every citation matches its reference. We return your document with track changes and a summary of the major fixes, so you also learn what to watch for next time.

[ What you get ]

  • Line-by-line grammar and syntax correction
  • Clarity and flow improvements
  • Citation and reference consistency check
  • Track-changes document so you see all edits
  • Formatting to journal or university style
  • Fast turnaround for urgent deadlines

[ FAQ ]

Will you change my meaning?
Never. We polish language only. Your arguments, data and conclusions stay exactly yours.
Do you work with non-native English writers?
Yes, most of our editing clients are. We specialize in making their writing read naturally.
What file formats do you accept?
Word documents primarily. We can also work with LaTeX, Google Docs and PDFs.
