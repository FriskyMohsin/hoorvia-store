How does bidding work?
Pick a service, fill the bid form with your project details and the budget you can pay, then send it via WhatsApp. We review every bid personally and reply with acceptance or a counter-offer — usually within 24 hours.
Why no fixed prices?
Every project is different. Bidding keeps things fair: you never overpay for simple work, and we can price complex work properly. You state your budget upfront, so there are no surprises.
How fast will I get a response?
We aim to respond to every bid within 24 hours, often much faster during business hours. Urgent project? Mention your deadline in the bid details.
Do you really deliver all 100+ services yourself?
Yes. Hoorvia Store is a curated expert practice, not a marketplace. Every project is handled directly, which means consistent quality and one point of contact.
What if my budget is small?
Bid anyway. Smaller, well-defined projects are welcome, and we will tell you honestly what your budget can get. No pressure, no upsells.
